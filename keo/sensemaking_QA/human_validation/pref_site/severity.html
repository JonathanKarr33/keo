Triple —

Class definitions (FAA AC 25.1309 / SAE ARP4761 style)
  • Catastrophic — Failure conditions that would prevent continued safe flight and landing; multiple fatalities and/or loss of the airplane are plausible.
  • Hazardous — Large reduction in safety margins or functional capability; physical distress or excessive workload such that the flight crew cannot be relied upon to perform tasks accurately or completely; serious or fatal injury to a relatively small number of occupants plausible.
  • Major — Significant reduction in safety margins or functional capability; significant increase in crew workload or conditions impairing crew efficiency; physical distress to occupants, including injuries.
  • Minor — Slight reduction in safety margins or slight increase in crew workload; some physical discomfort to occupants; routine crew actions suffice.
  • No safety effect (none) — No effect on safety; no effect on operational capability or crew workload beyond ordinary maintenance inconvenience.
